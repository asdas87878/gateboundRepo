# The Haven: ChatGPT concept art prompts

Each prompt turns one of our plain renders into a painting without moving anything big.

**How to use them**
1. Start a new chat for each view.
2. Attach the render from `art/haven/composition/` (the single image, not the sheet).
3. Attach `art/kit/props/compare_sheet.jpg` too: it shows the paint style we've approved.
4. Paste the style paragraph, then that view's line.

## Style paragraph (paste into every prompt)

> Paint over the first image as a piece of concept art for a stylized fantasy game. Keep its composition exactly: the same camera, horizon line, and the position and size of every large shape. The second image only shows the paint style to match, not its objects. Style: a hand-painted illustration in the look of a soft anime background painting. Chunky, simple, slightly crooked shapes with nothing perfectly straight or level. Crisp paint with soft, pale cream highlights along the edges. Warm golden light, shadows leaning plum and blue, never black or grey. Calm, natural colours, with the sky clear and the distance fading into soft haze. The floating islands in the sky stay exactly where they are and keep their colours, because each colour is a region: green grass, golden meadow, dark teal marsh, violet fae, red-orange desert, icy snow. Give each one rough rock underneath, with its grass, sand or snow spilling over the rim. No text, no interface, no crowds, no new large structures. You may add small details, rock ledges, steps, low terraces, paths, plants and lanterns.

## View lines

**A. Your plot** (`A_your_plot.png`)
> The red figure is one small adventurer, the same size and in the same place. We stand on a small floating grass island, our home. The grey blocks are small timber cottages and the pale circles are plots of ground, some empty and pegged out with string, one a vegetable garden. Past them a bridge leads to a larger floating island, where the tall stone tower rises. The sky beyond is full of other floating islands, one above another at many distances; the nearest has a giant tree.

**B. The bridge** (`B_bridge.png`)
> The red figure is one small adventurer walking along a sturdy old timber bridge with rope rails, high above a sea of clouds. Ahead is a large floating island with a small market and a stream that runs off its edge as a waterfall, falling into the clouds. Above it stands an ancient stone tower with ledges around each tier and a small glowing light on top. Distant floating islands hang in the sky behind it.

**C. The arch** (`C_arch_to_land.png`)
> The red figure is one small adventurer, seen from behind. Three huge ancient stone arches stand along the edge of the island. The middle arch is awake, its top glowing soft blue, and it perfectly frames a floating island with a giant tree in the distance. The arches on either side are dark and sealed. Other floating islands drift beyond them above a sea of clouds.

**D. The promenade** (`D_promenade.png`)
> The red figure is one small adventurer. A curved stone walkway follows the island's edge, lined with a row of huge ancient arches curving away into the distance. One arch has a soft blue glow; the rest are dark. Each one looks out at its own floating island. On the right is the massive stone base of the tower. The green is a lawn with worn paths.

**E. Your neighbour** (`E_neighbours.png`)
> The red figure is one small adventurer on the edge of their floating island, looking across a gap at a neighbour's island. That island is a thriving little hamlet of timber cottages with smoke from a chimney, a workshop and gardens. Further islands behind it have fewer buildings. Bridges connect them, and a sea of clouds lies far below.

**F. The whole Haven** (`F_establishing.png`)
> A wide view from high in the air of a floating settlement above a sea of clouds. In the middle is a large island with a tall stone tower. Eight smaller grass islands surround it in a crescent, each joined to it by a timber bridge, with cottages on them. A waterfall pours off the main island into the clouds. Beyond, dozens of floating islands hang in columns up the sky, one above another: a green one with a giant tree, a golden one with a windmill, snowy peaks, a dark marsh, violet mushroom islands, red desert mesas, each fading further into the haze.

**G. The sky of Lands** (`G_sky.png`)
> The red figure is one small adventurer at the edge of a stone walkway, looking out and up. The sky ahead is the hero of this picture: dozens of floating islands, stacked one above another into the far distance, fading into the haze. The nearest has a giant tree and a waterfall pouring into a sea of clouds below. The furthest, high and dead ahead, is a tiny pale snowy peak: the end of the journey. This should make you want to go to all of them.

## Follow-ups worth asking for

- **Your plot at three stages:** take your favourite result of A and ask: "Same view. Show it at the very start: one tent, empty plots." Then: "Same view, later: three cottages and a garden." Then: "Same view, fully built: a thriving hamlet with a forge, a workshop and a tall storehouse."
- **More height:** "Same view, with more changes of level on the island: steps and low rock terraces." Only the concept art needs this yet; I'll carry what you like back into the layout.
