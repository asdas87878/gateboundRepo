# Travel

## Decided (the user, 2026-11-08)
- **Easy to get home and to the Lands:** from the UI, and from a physical landmark at the base.
- **Not so easy that it breaks the balance.**
- **The worry:** going all the way home to cash in may take too long and put players off.

## How others do it (researched 2026-11-08)
- **Bee Swarm:**
  - you walk back to your hive to turn pollen into honey;
  - relief is sold as bigger bags, Instant Converters around the map (a ticket and a cooldown) and portable Micro-Converters (rare,
    up to 15);
  - its fields sit behind gates that open by how many bees you own.
- **Elsewhere:** bigger-backpack passes, teleport menus, sell-anywhere and auto-sell perks. Pet Simulator skips depositing.

## Ideas on the table (Claude, not agreed)
- **A Recall button**, with a 3-second wind-up that taking damage cancels. Plus a waystone at each Land's camp.
- **A ring of portals at the base**, one per Land, lit as they unlock. It's the physical landmark and a progress display in one,
  and it drops you at the Land's camp.
- **Target:** home and back in under 15 seconds.
- **Relief later:**
  - bigger packs;
  - a Courier charm;
  - rare carry-home scrolls;
  - a camp trader;
  - Robux convenience passes.
