# Crafting and the forge

## Decided (the user, 2026-11-08)
- **Weapons are crafted from specific ingredients in combinations.** This is where the game diverges from a pure numbers simulator.
- **The forge station** crafts, smelts, exchanges and creates new things.
- **The forge's tiers cap the tools you can make.** A tier can need a quest ingredient, for example a mithril anvil: you go on a
  hunt for the ingredient to upgrade it.
- **The crafting UI is cool to use:** a bit hard to figure out at first, but feasible to figure out.

## Ideas on the table (Claude, not agreed)
- **Readable recipes:** each Land drops 2 or 3 ingredients, and a recipe uses at most 3 kinds. The forge shows exactly what's
  missing and where it drops.
- **Forge tiers need a boss ingredient**, which ties base progress to Land progress on its own.
- **Gold fees:** every craft and smelt costs a little gold besides the ingredients.
- **A locked display case at the forge** shows the next great weapon and its recipe ("needs: Ember Core, Ash Fields").
