# Combat

## Decided
- **The start:** a basic sword against basic enemies, and a clear sense of the cool stuff you're missing ([First minutes](first-minutes.md)).
- **Drops:** XP, gold, gems and specific ingredients.
- **Danger:** you can always walk into a Land too strong for you and be killed. Defeat gives you something to strive for. The
  challenge must suit a beginner's skill (the user, 2026-11-08).
- **Kill bars (section 14):** a farming area has a kill bar, and a full bar spawns a mini-boss and a chest.
- **Bosses:** each Land has one, with signature drops.

## Ideas on the table (Claude, not agreed)
- **A toy in every field:** one twist per enemy type.
- **Speed rewards:** kill-fast multipliers with VFX.
- **Rare finds:** 1-in-5,000 golden enemies (section 14's list), and loot fountains.

## Open
- The weapon types and how they feel.
- Melee only, or ranged too.
- Bigger enemy packs, after a performance test (section 14).
