# Charms

## Decided (the user, 2026-11-08)
- **Charms work like pets:** you equip them for specific bonuses, for example +20 armour and lifesteal on your hits.
- **Slots:** about 3 to start, and you gain more as you go.
- **The best charms** come from hard content.

## Ideas on the table (Claude, not agreed)
- **Gems:** charms are bought, fused and rerolled with gems, and gems unlock slots.
- **Rebirth:** a rebirth-only charm slot ([Rebirth](rebirth.md)).
- **Courier charm:** sends ingredients home now and then ([Travel](travel.md)).
- **Display case:** charms shown in a case at the base, for showing off.
