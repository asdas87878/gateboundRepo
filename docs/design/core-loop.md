# Core loop

## Decided (the user, 2026-11-08)
The simulator loop is the foundation:
1. Kill enemies in a Land and gain resources.
2. Take them home.
3. Work them into better tools.
4. Farm stronger things faster.

- **Replayability is the next tool:** a sweeter fruit always hangs just out of reach.
- **The twist:** better tools need specific ingredients in combinations, not just a bigger number ([Crafting](crafting.md)).
- **Four ways to get stronger:** weapons, [abilities and stats](abilities-and-stats.md), and [charms](charms.md).
- **The feel:** bright, full of VFX, clear progression, satisfying rewards. Coins visibly fly into your inventory with good sounds,
  and depositing gives instant feedback.

## Ideas on the table (Claude, not agreed)
- **The next upgrade must always be nameable**, and early on about 5 to 15 minutes away.
- **Every upgrade is felt at once:** faster kills, a bigger number, a new effect. A training dummy at home shows it.
- **Split the drops:** XP, gold and gems go straight to you; only ingredients fill the pack. A full pack is the signal to go home,
  and the deposit is the big moment, when the forge lights up with what you can now craft.
