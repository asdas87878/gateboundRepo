# The first minutes

## Decided (the user, 2026-11-08)
- **No long cutscene.** A friend who's told to hop on must be playing at once.
- **Clear but intriguing.** How the game works must be clear, but still a little mysterious.
- **Show what they're missing:** you start with a basic sword and basic enemies, and should clearly see the cool weapons you don't
  have yet.

## Ideas on the table (Claude, not agreed)
- **The first kill pays within 10 seconds:** you spawn at your plot with the sword, and weak enemies wait just outside the gate.
- **Teach one thing at a time, only when it's needed:**
  - first full pack: the Recall button glows;
  - first deposit: the forge lights up;
  - first craft: hit the dummy.
- **Mystery:** a sealed gate at the base's edge, a huge silhouette in the sky, lore in item descriptions.
- **The pull of what's missing:**
  - other players' flashy gear, the strongest pull;
  - a locked display case at the forge;
  - the next Land always in view.
- **Friends:** joining a friend puts you next to them.
