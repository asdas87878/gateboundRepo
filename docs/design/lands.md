# The Lands

## Decided
- **About 30 Lands** in six themes of five, each with its own boss and twist (2026-11-06; [the list](../lands/lands-list.md)).
- **Inside a Land (the user, 2026-11-08):** farming stations of basic enemies, then a layer of tougher enemies on the way to a boss.
  The size of a Land is undecided. The farming must stay fun and not over-complicated.
- **Entering:** you can always walk into a Land too strong for you and be killed.
- **Building them (section 14):**
  - composed sets joined by sight-blocking necks;
  - one locked camera per set, matched to concept art;
  - edges that fall away, with other islands in view.
- **The user blocks out Lands by hand** in Studio.
- **Views (the user on Land 1, 2026-11-06):** too much empty space. Every view needs things that block the line of sight, and grand
  things in the sky. Before building more, list the assets needed and study the user's reference videos.

## Ideas on the table (Claude, not agreed)
- **Keep each Land small:** 2 or 3 fields, one path, one boss.
- **Ingredients:** 2 or 3 per Land.

## Open
- Land size, now that farming is the core.
- Open or locked progression ([Travel](travel.md)).
