# The base and town hall

## Decided (the user, 2026-11-08, and section 14)
- **What the base is for:** crafting, upgrading, personalising, and chilling.
- **It's upgradable, and some tools need a higher-level base.**
- **The stations:** a forge station ([Crafting](crafting.md)), NPC vendor hubs like Terraria (rescued NPCs live there), and extra
  scenery.
- **The town hall:** its role is undecided. It must not be only a limiter; it needs real rewards of its own. Forge or town hall
  first is an open choice.
- **The shared Haven (section 14):** the base is one plot among players' plots, so you see others' bases.

## Ideas on the table (Claude, not agreed)
- **Separate jobs.** The forge is power. The town hall is your home's level, and every level gives something you feel:
  - a building slot or more room;
  - a house for one more rescued NPC;
  - more storage;
  - a passive-income building;
  - a charm slot;
  - more portals in the travel ring.
- **The hall grows visibly**, from camp to hall to keep to castle.
- **Its only limit:** a station can't be more than one tier above the hall.
- **From tycoons:**
  - build buttons, so buildings pop into place;
  - NPC workers who produce while you're out (section 14's "home keeps working");
  - a trophy hall for boss trophies, weapon racks and charm cases;
  - a training dummy;
  - a gold pile in the vault that grows;
  - decor as loot, a daily chest, visiting and liking friends' bases.

## Open
- Set plots or free building (section 14).
- The town hall's role.
- Base size.
