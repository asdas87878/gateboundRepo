# The 30 Lands (Proposed 2026-11-06)

Six themes of five. Inside a theme the kit and enemy family are shared, and each Land changes one big thing and has its own boss. All names are working names. Positions in the sky: `art/haven/layout.json`.

**Theme 2, High Meadows,** is my reading of the user's "top grass": higher, lighter, windier grass. Brightwheat moved into it from being its own Land.

## What it costs to make
At 5-6 hand-composed sets per Land, 30 Lands would be 150-180 sets. Proposed instead:
- **The first Land of each theme is the showpiece** (5-6 sets). The other four get 2-3 sets and a boss arena, using the kit the showpiece built. That's about 105 sets.
- **Release in waves.** Launch with Greenreach and High Meadows (10 Lands), then add a theme per update. Every Land is in the sky from day one, so players see what's coming.
- **Each new theme needs:** its rock, ground and foliage kit, an enemy family and five bosses.
